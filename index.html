Industrial Fabrication FAQ – Nagpur

The questions customers ask us most about our fabrication products and services.

What Industrial Fabrication Services does Mahindo Fab Tech provide in Nagpur?

We provide industrial fabrication, storage tanks, structural fabrication, petroleum tankers, tanker refurbishment and industrial equipment fabrication, along with engineering works and container houses, all made to your drawings and specifications.

What are Container Houses used for, and can they be customised?

Our container houses are used as site offices, staff accommodation, security and site cabins, and commercial units. They are customizable, so size, layout, doors, windows, partitions and finishing are planned with you before fabrication starts.

What details should I share to get a Container House quotation?

Share the intended use (office, accommodation, cabin or commercial), the size or number of units you need, your preferred layout, finishing requirements and the delivery location by call, WhatsApp or the enquiry form. We will then discuss scope and quotation with you.

Can you manufacture a Petroleum Tanker as per my specification?

Yes. We manufacture heavy-duty petroleum tankers built for safety, durability and transportation requirements, working from your drawings, vehicle details and applicable regulatory requirements.

Do you repair, modify or refurbish old tankers?

Yes. Our Tanker Refurbishment service covers professional repair, modification, restoration and refurbishment to extend service life and performance. Feasibility is assessed after inspecting the tanker.

Do you undertake Structural and Industrial Equipment Fabrication?

Yes. We fabricate structural work such as industrial frames, sheds, platforms and supports, as well as custom industrial equipment like vessels, skids and specialized assemblies as per your project requirements.

What information should I share to get a fabrication quotation?

Share your drawing or sketch, dimensions, material, quantity, application and delivery location by call, WhatsApp or the enquiry form. The more detail you provide, the faster we can discuss scope and quotation.
